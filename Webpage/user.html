How It Works
Park in 4 Simple Steps

From reservation to payment — the entire process is handled through ParkSmart.

1
Reserve a Space

Browse available parking spaces in real time and reserve the one you want. Set your duration and confirm in seconds.

2
Get Your QR Code

After confirming your reservation, you receive a unique QR code. Screenshot it — you will need it at the entrance.

3
Check In at Barrier

Show your QR code to the attendant at the entrance barrier. They scan it to verify and activate your session.

4
Park & Pay

Park your vehicle. When you are done, drive out and end your session from My Bookings. Your total is calculated automatically.
